## Brief description
A dark-themed, browser-based twist on Tetris: arrange three colorful triangle families on a 10 × 20 grid against a countdown timer. Pieces do not fall while you position them. Fill complete rows, avoid reaching the top, and keep building as the timer gradually speeds up.

## Instructions for use
Press Enter or select **Let’s play** to start. Use **Left**, **Right**, and **Down** to move, **Up** to rotate counterclockwise, **W** to rotate clockwise, **Space** to place, and **C** to hold or swap a piece once per turn. Hold a movement key for continuous movement. **P** pauses; **F** toggles fullscreen. On mobile, use the on-screen movement, rotation, Hold, and Place buttons beneath the board, plus Pause just below the board.

Pieces must touch the floor or another piece without overlapping. Space, Place, and timer expiry keep a legal current position unchanged; otherwise they place the piece at the highest legal position supported from below in the same column. The timer starts at ten seconds and shortens slightly after each placement. Clear full rows to earn points; touching the top or having no supported position for an illegal placement ends the game. Your personal best is saved in your browser.
